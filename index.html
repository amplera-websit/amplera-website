We develop the technology. Then we develop it with you.

Amplera develops the core technologies of molecular detection — CRISPR chemistries, isothermal amplification, multiplex PCR, and the computational design engine behind them.

The same platforms we advance in our own research are the ones we bring to a partner’s program. We deliver that work under contract, to your timeline and your documentation standards — but you are not buying a set of steps. You are building on technology we own.

Client

Client retained the IP.

A shrimp-health company needed to screen for several high-impact pathogens that present with overlapping signs — a problem single-target tests solve slowly and expensively. We designed a probe-based four-target multiplex real-time PCR assay with an internal amplification control, optimized extraction for tissue, soil and water matrices, and carried it through analytical validation.
